# Tomato Truth Foundation

## Article 3, Section 1: The Primacy of Sauce

### 1.1 Foundational Role

The tomato sauce serves as the substrate upon which all other pizza elements are built, providing:
- Moisture regulation
- Flavor foundation
- Structural adhesion layer
- pH balance (ideal range: 4.0-4.6)

### 1.2 Tomato Selection Standards

Sauce shall be prepared from tomatoes meeting the following criteria:
- San Marzano DOP or equivalent quality
- Ripeness index: 85-95%
- Sugar content (Brix): 4.5-5.5
- Acidity: 0.35-0.45%

### 1.3 Processing Requirements

The transformation of tomatoes into sauce must preserve essential compounds:
- Lycopene content maintained >80%
- Umami compounds (glutamate) enhanced through proper cooking
- Texture: smooth with minimal seeds and skin fragments

### 1.4 Application Depth

Sauce depth shall be maintained at:
- Minimum: 2mm
- Optimal: 3-4mm
- Maximum: 6mm

Exceeding these bounds disrupts the cheese-to-sauce ratio and compromises structural integrity.

### 1.5 Distribution Pattern

Sauce application shall follow the spiral or concentric circle pattern, ensuring even coverage while maintaining a 1-2cm border for crust formation.

## Truth Principles

The sauce is the truth upon which the pizza is built. Deviation from these standards undermines the entire ontological structure.
