# Topping Telos

## Article 3, Section 2: The Purpose and End of Toppings

### 2.1 Teleological Framework

Each topping serves a specific purpose (telos) within the pizza ontology:
- **Vegetables**: Provide textural contrast and nutritional balance
- **Meats**: Contribute umami depth and protein density
- **Herbs**: Offer aromatic complexity and flavor brightness
- **Additional Cheeses**: Enhance flavor layering and textural variety

### 2.2 Ordering Principle

Toppings shall be layered according to their cooking requirements:

1. **Base Layer** (direct on sauce): Hardy vegetables (onions, peppers)
2. **Middle Layer**: Cheese matrix
3. **Upper Layer**: Pre-cooked meats, delicate items
4. **Post-Bake Layer**: Fresh herbs, finishing oils

### 2.3 Quantity Constraints

Maximum topping coverage shall not exceed:
- Total topping mass: 200g per 30cm pizza
- Individual topping limit: 50g per variety
- Total topping varieties: 5 maximum for coherence

### 2.4 Moisture Management

Toppings with high moisture content (>75%) must be:
- Pre-cooked to reduce water content by 30-40%
- Distributed sparingly to prevent sauce dilution
- Positioned to allow steam escape during baking

### 2.5 Thermal Compatibility

All toppings must withstand baking temperatures of 230-260°C without:
- Excessive charring (>20% surface area)
- Complete dehydration (residual moisture <15%)
- Structural disintegration

### 2.6 The Principle of Complementarity

Each topping must complement rather than compete with the fundamental trinity of dough, sauce, and cheese. Toppings exist to fulfill and enhance, not to dominate.

## Telos Violations

Excessive toppings or improper ordering disrupts the intended purpose and creates ontological chaos within the pizza field.
