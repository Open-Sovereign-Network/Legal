# Mozzarella Mandate

## Article 1, Section 2: Requirements for Cheese Selection

### 2.1 Purity Standards

All mozzarella used within the OSN pizza ontology must meet minimum purity standards of 95% milk-derived proteins and lipids.

### 2.2 Moisture Content

The mandated moisture content shall be maintained between 45-55% to ensure optimal melting and stretching characteristics.

### 2.3 Distribution Requirements

Cheese must be distributed evenly across the pizza surface with a tolerance of ±10% variance per quadrant to maintain coherence across the entire ontological field.

### 2.4 Layering Protocol

When multiple cheese types are employed, mozzarella shall occupy the primary layer position, directly atop the sauce substrate, to establish the foundational cheese matrix.

### 2.5 Fresh vs. Low-Moisture Distinction

- **Fresh Mozzarella**: Reserved for post-bake applications and specialty preparations
- **Low-Moisture Mozzarella**: Designated for standard baking procedures to achieve optimal browning and texture

## Compliance

Adherence to the Mozzarella Mandate is non-negotiable and forms the basis of all cheese-related jurisprudence within this framework.
